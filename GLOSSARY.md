# Orbit

Names for the day list and for habits: what was owed, what was done, what could not be done, and what was left undone on purpose.

## Language

**Habit**:
A practice owed on chosen weekdays.
_Avoid_: task, todo, goal

**Skip**:
A scheduled habit day you could not do.
_Avoid_: rest, sick day, lazy, color

**Slack**:
A scheduled day you were able to do and chose not to.
_Avoid_: lazy, rest, skip, color

**Random week task**:
A week-template task that belongs to one weekday of a given week.
_Avoid_: daily task, everyday task
